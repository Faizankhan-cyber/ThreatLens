# ThreatLens

### Is that link safe — or a trap?

ThreatLens is a digital threat detection platform designed to help users identify suspicious links, phishing attempts, scams, and other potentially harmful digital content before interacting with them.

It provides a simple, user-friendly interface for checking different types of suspicious digital content in one place.

## 🎯 The Problem

Online scams and phishing attacks are becoming harder to recognize.

Attackers commonly use:

- Fake banking messages
- UPI payment scams
- KYC verification scams
- Fake delivery notifications
- Malicious links
- Social engineering
- Fake login pages
- Suspicious emails and messages

For many users, it can be difficult to determine whether something is legitimate before clicking a link or sharing information.

ThreatLens aims to make that first security check simple.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

ThreatLens provides a centralized digital safety interface where users can submit suspicious content and analyse it before interacting with it.

The platform is built around a simple workflow:

```text
Submit suspicious content
        ↓
Threat analysis
        ↓
Risk identification
        ↓
Understand the threat
        ↓
Make a safer decision '''


## ✨ Features
🔗 URL Scanner

Analyse suspicious URLs and identify potential phishing or malicious-link indicators.

💬 Message Scanner

Check suspicious messages for potential scam and phishing patterns.

📧 Email Scanner

Analyse suspicious emails before interacting with links or requests inside them.

🖼️ Screenshot Scanner

Use screenshots as an additional source of threat investigation.

🔳 QR Scanner

Inspect suspicious QR codes before scanning or interacting with them.

☎️ Phone Checker

Investigate suspicious phone numbers and potential scam indicators.
